## Models

- Primary: configurable via `OPENAI_PRIMARY_MODEL` (default `gpt-5.6-luna`) — prefer free/advanced daily quota
- Fallback: `OPENAI_FALLBACK_MODEL` (default `gpt-4o-mini`) — paid wallet, minimal spend
- Voice: **browser Web Speech API** (no Whisper spend)
